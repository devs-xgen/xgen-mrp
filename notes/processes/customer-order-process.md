# Customer Order Process

**Purpose:** Complete guide to managing customer orders from receipt to production planning  
**Process Owner:** Admin/Sales Team  
**Key Stakeholders:** Customers, Production Planning, Material Management  
**Last Updated:** January 20, 2025

## 🎯 Process Overview

The Customer Order Process manages the complete lifecycle of customer orders, from initial receipt through production planning and delivery coordination. This process ensures accurate order capture, efficient production planning, and excellent customer service.

### **Process Objectives**
- **Accurate Order Capture** - Ensure all customer requirements are correctly recorded
- **Efficient Processing** - Process orders quickly and efficiently
- **Production Integration** - Seamlessly integrate orders with production planning
- **Customer Communication** - Keep customers informed throughout the process
- **Quality Assurance** - Ensure orders meet quality and delivery requirements

### **Process Scope**
- Customer order receipt and validation
- Order entry and documentation
- Production planning and scheduling
- Material requirement planning
- Customer communication and updates
- Order tracking and status management

## 📋 Process Steps

### **Step 1: Customer Order Receipt**

#### **Activities**
1. **Order Reception** - Receive order through various channels (phone, email, web, in-person)
2. **Initial Review** - Quick review of order feasibility and completeness
3. **Customer Verification** - Verify customer information and credit status
4. **Order Documentation** - Document all order details and requirements

#### **Inputs**
- Customer order request
- Product specifications
- Delivery requirements
- Pricing information

#### **Outputs**
- Documented customer order
- Initial feasibility assessment
- Customer verification status

#### **Responsible Roles**
- **Primary:** Admin/Sales Representative
- **Supporting:** Customer Service, Credit Department

### **Step 2: Order Validation and Entry**

#### **Activities**
1. **Product Verification** - Confirm product availability and specifications
2. **Quantity Validation** - Verify order quantities against capacity
3. **Delivery Date Check** - Confirm feasibility of requested delivery dates
4. **Pricing Confirmation** - Verify pricing and terms
5. **System Entry** - Enter complete order into the system

#### **System Actions**
- Create customer order record
- Validate product information
- Check customer credit limits
- Calculate order totals and taxes
- Generate order confirmation

#### **Quality Checks**
- All required fields completed
- Product specifications match customer requirements
- Delivery dates are realistic
- Pricing is accurate and approved

#### **Responsible Roles**
- **Primary:** Admin/Order Entry Specialist
- **Supporting:** Sales Team, Product Management

### **Step 3: Production Planning Integration**

#### **Activities**
1. **Production Assessment** - Evaluate production requirements for the order
2. **Capacity Check** - Verify production capacity availability
3. **Material Planning** - Assess material requirements and availability
4. **Schedule Planning** - Plan production timing and sequencing
5. **Production Order Creation** - Generate production orders if needed

#### **System Integration**
- Automatic material requirement calculation
- Production capacity checking
- Material availability verification
- Production order generation
- Schedule optimization

#### **Decision Points**
- **Make-to-Stock** - Use existing inventory if available
- **Make-to-Order** - Create production order for custom requirements
- **Partial Fulfillment** - Handle partial availability scenarios
- **Schedule Adjustment** - Modify delivery dates if needed

#### **Responsible Roles**
- **Primary:** Production Planner/Admin
- **Supporting:** Material Manager, Production Manager

### **Step 4: Material Requirements Planning**

#### **Activities**
1. **BOM Analysis** - Analyze bill of materials for required products
2. **Inventory Check** - Check current material inventory levels
3. **Requirement Calculation** - Calculate total material needs
4. **Availability Assessment** - Assess material availability vs requirements
5. **Procurement Planning** - Plan material procurement if needed

#### **System Functions**
- Automatic BOM explosion
- Real-time inventory checking
- Material requirement calculation
- Supplier lead time consideration
- Purchase order generation

#### **Material Scenarios**
- **Available** - Sufficient materials in stock
- **Partial** - Some materials available, others need procurement
- **Unavailable** - Materials need to be ordered from suppliers
- **Substitute** - Alternative materials may be used

#### **Responsible Roles**
- **Primary:** Material Planner/Admin
- **Supporting:** Purchasing, Inventory Management

### **Step 5: Order Confirmation and Communication**

#### **Activities**
1. **Order Review** - Final review of complete order details
2. **Customer Confirmation** - Confirm order details with customer
3. **Delivery Schedule** - Confirm delivery dates and logistics
4. **Documentation** - Generate order confirmation documents
5. **Internal Communication** - Communicate order to relevant teams

#### **Customer Communication**
- Order confirmation with all details
- Delivery schedule and expectations
- Contact information for questions
- Order tracking information
- Payment terms and conditions

#### **Internal Communication**
- Production team notification
- Material planning notification
- Quality team notification
- Delivery team notification

#### **Responsible Roles**
- **Primary:** Customer Service/Admin
- **Supporting:** All relevant departments

### **Step 6: Order Tracking and Management**

#### **Activities**
1. **Status Monitoring** - Monitor order progress through production
2. **Customer Updates** - Provide regular updates to customers
3. **Issue Management** - Handle any issues or changes
4. **Schedule Management** - Manage delivery schedules and changes
5. **Completion Tracking** - Track order through to completion

#### **Tracking Elements**
- Order status and progress
- Production progress
- Material availability
- Quality check status
- Delivery preparation
- Customer communication history

#### **Status Updates**
- **Order Received** - Order entered and confirmed
- **In Planning** - Production and material planning in progress
- **Scheduled** - Production scheduled and materials reserved
- **In Production** - Manufacturing in progress
- **Quality Check** - Quality inspection in progress
- **Ready for Delivery** - Order complete and ready for shipment
- **Delivered** - Order successfully delivered to customer

#### **Responsible Roles**
- **Primary:** Customer Service/Admin
- **Supporting:** Production, Quality, Delivery teams

## 🔄 Process Flow Diagram

```
Customer Request → Order Validation → System Entry → 
Production Planning → Material Planning → Order Confirmation → 
Production Execution → Quality Control → Delivery → Completion
```

## 🚨 Common Issues and Solutions

### **Issue: Incomplete Order Information**
**Symptoms:** Missing product details, unclear specifications, incomplete customer information
**Solutions:**
- Implement order validation checklists
- Train staff on complete order capture
- Use system validation rules
- Follow up with customers for missing information

### **Issue: Unrealistic Delivery Dates**
**Symptoms:** Customer requests impossible delivery dates, production capacity conflicts
**Solutions:**
- Implement realistic delivery date calculation
- Communicate production lead times clearly
- Offer alternative delivery options
- Manage customer expectations proactively

### **Issue: Material Availability Problems**
**Symptoms:** Orders accepted without checking material availability, production delays
**Solutions:**
- Implement real-time material checking
- Integrate order entry with inventory system
- Communicate material constraints early
- Develop alternative material options

### **Issue: Poor Customer Communication**
**Symptoms:** Customers unaware of order status, complaints about lack of updates
**Solutions:**
- Implement automated status updates
- Train staff on customer communication
- Establish regular update schedules
- Provide customer self-service options

## 🎯 Best Practices

### **Order Capture Best Practices**
1. **Complete Information** - Capture all required information at order entry
2. **Customer Verification** - Always verify customer information and requirements
3. **Documentation** - Document all customer communications and requirements
4. **Confirmation** - Always confirm order details with customers
5. **System Integration** - Use integrated systems for accurate information

### **Production Integration Best Practices**
1. **Real-Time Checking** - Check production capacity and material availability in real-time
2. **Realistic Scheduling** - Provide realistic delivery dates based on actual capacity
3. **Proactive Communication** - Communicate potential issues early
4. **Flexible Planning** - Build flexibility into production plans
5. **Continuous Monitoring** - Monitor order progress continuously

### **Customer Service Best Practices**
1. **Proactive Communication** - Keep customers informed without them asking
2. **Clear Expectations** - Set clear expectations for delivery and quality
3. **Issue Resolution** - Resolve issues quickly and effectively
4. **Feedback Collection** - Collect and act on customer feedback
5. **Relationship Building** - Build long-term customer relationships
