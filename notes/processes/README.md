# Business Process Documentation

**Purpose:** Complete guide to key business processes in the XGen MRP system  
**Audience:** All users, managers, and stakeholders  
**Last Updated:** January 20, 2025

## 🔄 Overview of Business Processes

The XGen MRP system manages several interconnected business processes that work together to deliver efficient manufacturing operations. These processes ensure smooth flow from customer orders through production to final delivery.

## 📋 Core Business Processes

### **1. Customer Order to Production Process**
The complete journey from receiving a customer order to starting production.

### **2. Material Resource Planning (MRP) Process**
Managing material requirements, procurement, and allocation for production.

### **3. Production Execution Process**
The sequential workflow of manufacturing operations from start to completion.

### **4. Quality Control Process**
Ensuring product quality through systematic inspections and controls.

### **5. Delivery and Fulfillment Process**
Getting finished products to customers efficiently and on time.

### **6. Inventory Management Process**
Maintaining optimal inventory levels and tracking all material movements.

## 🎯 Process Integration and Dependencies

### **Process Flow Overview**
```
Customer Order → Material Planning → Production Planning → 
Production Execution → Quality Control → Delivery → Completion
```

### **Key Integration Points**
- **Order-to-Production** - Customer orders trigger production planning
- **Material-to-Production** - Material availability enables production start
- **Production-to-Quality** - Completed production triggers quality checks
- **Quality-to-Delivery** - Quality approval enables delivery preparation
- **Delivery-to-Completion** - Successful delivery completes the order cycle
