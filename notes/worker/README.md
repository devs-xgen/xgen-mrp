# Worker User Documentation

**Role:** Production Worker & Shop Floor Personnel  
**Access Level:** Production Tasks and Inventory Management  
**Primary Responsibilities:** Execute production tasks, manage materials, and report progress

## 👷‍♂️ Worker Role Overview

Worker users are the backbone of production operations and are responsible for:
- **Production Execution** - Carry out manufacturing tasks and operations
- **Task Management** - Track and update progress on assigned work
- **Material Handling** - Manage material consumption and inventory
- **Quality Participation** - Support quality control processes
- **Progress Reporting** - Keep management informed of production status

## 🎯 Key Responsibilities

### **Daily Production Tasks**
- Execute assigned production operations
- Update task status and progress in real-time
- Record material consumption and usage
- Report any issues or problems encountered
- Coordinate with team members and supervisors

### **Material Management**
- Track material usage during production
- Report material shortages or quality issues
- Request additional materials when needed
- Maintain accurate inventory records
- Handle material transfers between locations

### **Quality Support**
- Follow quality procedures and standards
- Report quality issues immediately
- Support quality inspections when required
- Maintain clean and organized work areas
- Follow safety protocols and procedures

### **Communication and Coordination**
- Update supervisors on progress and issues
- Coordinate with other workers and departments
- Participate in team meetings and briefings
- Provide feedback on processes and improvements
- Support training of new team members

## 📋 Daily Worker Workflow

### **Starting Your Shift**
1. **Check Dashboard** - Review tasks assigned for the day
2. **Review Priorities** - Identify urgent or high-priority tasks
3. **Check Materials** - Ensure you have materials needed for tasks
4. **Safety Check** - Verify equipment and work area safety
5. **Team Briefing** - Attend shift briefing if scheduled

### **During Production**
1. **Start Tasks** - Begin working on assigned tasks
2. **Update Progress** - Regularly update task progress
3. **Track Materials** - Record material consumption as you work
4. **Monitor Quality** - Check quality during production
5. **Report Issues** - Immediately report any problems

### **Task Completion**
1. **Final Quality Check** - Verify work meets quality standards
2. **Complete Task** - Mark task as finished in the system
3. **Update Inventory** - Record final material usage
4. **Clean Work Area** - Maintain clean and organized workspace
5. **Prepare for Next Task** - Set up for next assigned task

### **End of Shift**
1. **Complete Updates** - Ensure all progress is recorded
2. **Report Issues** - Document any unresolved problems
3. **Handover Notes** - Communicate with next shift
4. **Equipment Shutdown** - Properly shut down equipment
5. **Final Safety Check** - Ensure work area is safe
