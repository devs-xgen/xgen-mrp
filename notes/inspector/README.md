# Inspector User Documentation

**Role:** Quality Control Inspector & Quality Assurance Personnel  
**Access Level:** Quality Management and Inspection Tasks  
**Primary Responsibilities:** Ensure product quality, conduct inspections, and maintain quality standards

## 🔍 Inspector Role Overview

Inspector users are responsible for maintaining product quality and ensuring compliance with standards:
- **Quality Inspections** - Conduct thorough product and material inspections
- **Quality Standards** - Ensure compliance with quality requirements
- **Issue Resolution** - Identify and resolve quality problems
- **Documentation** - Maintain detailed quality records
- **Continuous Improvement** - Contribute to quality improvement initiatives

## 🎯 Key Responsibilities

### **Quality Inspections**
- Perform incoming material inspections
- Conduct in-process quality checks during production
- Execute final product inspections before delivery
- Document inspection results and findings
- Approve or reject products based on quality standards

### **Quality Documentation**
- Maintain detailed inspection records
- Document quality issues and corrective actions
- Generate quality reports and certificates
- Track quality metrics and trends
- Ensure compliance with regulatory requirements

### **Issue Management**
- Identify quality problems and defects
- Investigate root causes of quality issues
- Recommend corrective and preventive actions
- Follow up on quality improvement initiatives
- Coordinate with production teams on quality matters

### **Standards Compliance**
- Ensure adherence to quality standards and specifications
- Verify compliance with industry regulations
- Maintain certification requirements
- Support quality audits and assessments
- Stay updated on quality standards and best practices

## 📋 Quality Inspection Process

### **Pre-Inspection Preparation**
1. **Review Requirements** - Understand quality standards and specifications
2. **Prepare Tools** - Gather necessary inspection equipment and tools
3. **Check Documentation** - Review production order and quality requirements
4. **Plan Inspection** - Determine inspection sequence and methods
5. **Safety Preparation** - Ensure safe inspection environment

### **Conducting Inspections**
1. **Initial Assessment** - Overall visual inspection of products
2. **Detailed Checks** - Systematic inspection of all quality criteria
3. **Measurements** - Take required measurements and tests
4. **Documentation** - Record all findings and observations
5. **Decision Making** - Determine pass/fail status based on standards

### **Post-Inspection Activities**
1. **Results Recording** - Enter inspection results into the system
2. **Quality Decision** - Approve or reject products based on findings
3. **Issue Reporting** - Report any quality problems found
4. **Documentation** - Complete all required quality documentation
5. **Communication** - Inform relevant teams of inspection results

### **Quality Issue Handling**
1. **Issue Identification** - Clearly identify and document quality problems
2. **Impact Assessment** - Determine scope and impact of quality issues
3. **Immediate Actions** - Take immediate steps to contain problems
4. **Investigation** - Conduct thorough investigation of root causes
5. **Resolution** - Implement corrective actions and verify effectiveness
