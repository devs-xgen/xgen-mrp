# P7.1: Product Management (Cross-Tenant)

**Priority:** 7 (Low - Enhancement)
**Estimated Effort:** Large (4-5 days)
**Status:** Not Started
**Category:** Product Management

---

## Overview

Implement comprehensive product and BOM management capabilities for SuperAdmin to manage products across all organizations, create master product catalogs, manage product variants, and oversee BOM configurations globally.

## Business Value

- **Master Catalog Management:** Maintain centralized product definitions
- **BOM Oversight:** View and manage BOMs across all organizations
- **Product Analytics:** Cross-tenant product performance insights
- **Quality Control:** Ensure BOM consistency and prevent errors
- **Bulk Operations:** Efficiently manage large product catalogs

## User Stories

### Story 1: View All Products (Cross-Tenant)
**As a** SuperAdmin
**I want to** view all products across all organizations
**So that** I can oversee the entire product catalog and identify issues

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- List all products with organization filtering
- Show product variants and SKUs
- Display BOM status (configured, missing, outdated)
- Filter by product type, category, status
- Search by name, SKU, or material
- Export product list to Excel/CSV
- Show product usage statistics

### Story 2: Product BOM Management
**As a** SuperAdmin
**I want to** view and edit product BOMs globally
**So that** I can fix BOM errors and ensure manufacturing accuracy

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- View BOM for any product/variant
- Edit BOM materials and quantities
- Compare BOMs across organizations
- Validate BOM completeness
- Check material availability
- Calculate total BOM cost
- Export BOM to Excel
- Audit BOM change history

### Story 3: Bulk Product Operations
**As a** SuperAdmin
**I want to** perform bulk operations on products
**So that** I can efficiently manage large catalogs

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Bulk update product prices
- Bulk update lead times
- Bulk archive/unarchive products
- Bulk BOM updates (e.g., replace material across all BOMs)
- Preview changes before applying
- Audit reason required
- Transaction safety (rollback on error)
- Summary report after operation

### Story 4: Product Analytics & Reports
**As a** SuperAdmin
**I want to** analyze product performance across organizations
**So that** I can identify best sellers, slow movers, and profitability

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Most produced products
- Products by revenue
- Products by profit margin
- Material usage by product
- Production time by product
- Quality issues by product
- Slow-moving products report
- BOM cost variance analysis

### Story 5: Master Product Templates
**As a** SuperAdmin
**I want to** create master product templates
**So that** organizations can quickly create standardized products

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Create product templates with default BOMs
- Template categories (shirts, pants, jackets)
- Template variants (sizes, colors)
- Organizations can instantiate templates
- Templates include standard materials
- Templates include default lead times
- Template usage tracking
- Update templates (notify affected orgs)
