# Inspector User Documentation

## Overview

The Inspector interface is designed for quality control professionals who ensure product quality, conduct inspections, manage defect tracking, and maintain compliance standards. Inspectors work across all stages of production to ensure products meet quality specifications and customer requirements.

## 🎯 Primary Responsibilities

- **Quality Inspections**: Conduct systematic quality checks at all production stages
- **Defect Management**: Identify, document, and track defects through resolution
- **Compliance Monitoring**: Ensure products meet regulatory and customer standards
- **Process Validation**: Verify production processes meet quality requirements
- **Documentation**: Maintain detailed inspection records and reports

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

### Inspector Dashboard
- **Inspection Queue**: Prioritized list of pending inspections
- **Quality Metrics**: Real-time quality performance indicators
- **Defect Trends**: Historical defect patterns and trending analysis
- **Compliance Status**: Current compliance standing and alerts
- **Team Performance**: Inspector team performance metrics

### Quick Actions
- **Start Inspection**: Begin new quality inspection
- **Report Defect**: Document quality issues immediately
- **Review History**: Access historical inspection data
- **Generate Report**: Create quality reports for stakeholders
- **Update Standards**: Modify inspection criteria as needed

## 🔍 Inspection Workflows

### Types of Inspections

#### Incoming Material Inspection
- **Purpose**: Verify quality of raw materials before production
- **Frequency**: Every incoming material lot
- **Key Checks**:
  - Material specifications compliance
  - Visual defects and damage
  - Dimensional accuracy
  - Color and texture consistency
  - Documentation completeness

#### In-Process Inspection
- **Purpose**: Monitor quality during production
- **Frequency**: At defined production milestones
- **Key Checks**:
  - Process parameter compliance
  - Product specifications adherence
  - Workmanship quality
  - Safety compliance
  - Documentation accuracy

#### Final Inspection
- **Purpose**: Verify final product quality before delivery
- **Frequency**: Every finished product
- **Key Checks**:
  - Complete product specifications
  - Final quality standards
  - Packaging compliance
  - Documentation completeness
  - Customer requirement verification

### Inspection Process

#### Pre-Inspection Setup
1. **Review Inspection Criteria**: Understand specific quality requirements
2. **Gather Tools**: Collect required inspection tools and equipment
3. **Access Documentation**: Review product specifications and standards
4. **Prepare Environment**: Ensure proper inspection environment
5. **Safety Check**: Verify safety protocols are in place

#### Conducting Inspections
1. **Sample Selection**: Select representative samples for inspection
2. **Systematic Check**: Follow defined inspection sequence
3. **Documentation**: Record all findings with photos when necessary
4. **Measurement Recording**: Document actual measurements vs. specifications
5. **Pass/Fail Determination**: Make clear pass/fail decisions

#### Post-Inspection Actions
1. **Result Documentation**: Complete inspection reports
2. **Defect Reporting**: Document any defects found
3. **Notification**: Inform relevant stakeholders of results
4. **Follow-up Actions**: Schedule any required follow-up inspections
5. **Record Keeping**: Maintain inspection records for traceability

## 📋 Defect Management

### Defect Classification

#### Critical Defects
- **Definition**: Defects that make product unusable or unsafe
- **Examples**: Safety hazards, complete functional failure
- **Action**: Immediate production stop and investigation
- **Documentation**: Detailed investigation report required

#### Major Defects
- **Definition**: Defects that significantly impact product quality
- **Examples**: Major visual defects, significant dimensional issues
- **Action**: Rework or scrap decision required
- **Documentation**: Detailed defect analysis and corrective action

#### Minor Defects
- **Definition**: Defects that have minimal impact on product quality
- **Examples**: Minor visual imperfections, slight color variations
- **Action**: Accept, rework, or scrap based on customer requirements
- **Documentation**: Standard defect reporting

### Defect Tracking Process

#### Defect Identification
1. **Visual Inspection**: Systematic visual examination
2. **Measurement**: Precise dimensional measurements
3. **Functional Testing**: Verify product functionality
4. **Comparison**: Compare against standards and samples
5. **Documentation**: Photograph and document all defects

#### Defect Recording
1. **Defect Code**: Assign standardized defect code
2. **Location**: Document exact defect location
3. **Severity**: Rate defect severity level
4. **Quantity**: Record number of defective units
5. **Root Cause**: Identify potential root cause

#### Defect Analysis
1. **Trend Analysis**: Identify defect patterns over time
2. **Root Cause Analysis**: Investigate underlying causes
3. **Corrective Actions**: Develop and implement fixes
4. **Prevention Measures**: Implement preventive actions
5. **Effectiveness Verification**: Verify corrective actions work

## 📊 Quality Metrics

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

#### Quality Rate
- **Calculation**: (Good Units / Total Units) × 100
- **Target**: ≥ 98% for all products
- **Monitoring**: Daily, weekly, and monthly tracking
- **Action**: Investigate when rate drops below target

#### Defect Rate
- **Calculation**: (Defective Units / Total Units) × 100
- **Target**: ≤ 2% for all products
- **Monitoring**: Real-time tracking and trending
- **Action**: Immediate investigation when rate increases

#### First Pass Yield
- **Calculation**: (Good Units First Inspection / Total Units Inspected) × 100
- **Target**: ≥ 95% for all products
- **Monitoring**: Continuous monitoring and improvement
- **Action**: Process improvement when yield drops

#### Customer Complaints
- **Tracking**: Number and type of customer complaints
- **Target**: Zero complaints related to quality
- **Monitoring**: Daily tracking and immediate response
- **Action**: Immediate investigation and corrective action
