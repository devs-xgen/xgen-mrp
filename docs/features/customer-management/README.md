# Customer Management Features

## Overview

The Customer Management system provides comprehensive tools for managing customer relationships, sales processes, order fulfillment, and customer service. This includes customer master data, sales order processing, delivery management, customer communication, and performance analytics.

## 👥 Customer Master Data Management

### Customer Profile Management

#### Basic Customer Information
- **Company Details**: Complete company information including legal name, address, and contact details
- **Contact Management**: Manage multiple contacts per customer with role-based access
- **Communication Preferences**: Set customer communication preferences and channels
- **Customer Classification**: Categorize customers by industry, size, and importance
- **Customer Status**: Track customer lifecycle stages from prospect to active/inactive

#### Customer Hierarchies
- **Parent-Child Relationships**: Manage complex customer organizational structures
- **Multi-location Support**: Track customers with multiple locations and subsidiaries
- **Bill-to/Ship-to Management**: Manage billing and shipping addresses separately
- **Contact Hierarchies**: Organize customer contacts by department and role
- **Decision Maker Mapping**: Map key decision makers and influencers

### Customer Financial Management

#### Credit Management
- **Credit Limits**: Set and monitor customer credit limits
- **Credit Terms**: Manage customer-specific payment terms and conditions
- **Credit History**: Track customer payment history and creditworthiness
- **Risk Assessment**: Assess customer financial risk and payment reliability
- **Credit Alerts**: Automated alerts for credit limit breaches and overdue payments

#### Pricing Management
- **Customer-specific Pricing**: Set individual customer pricing agreements
- **Volume Discounts**: Configure volume-based pricing discounts
- **Contract Pricing**: Manage long-term pricing contracts and agreements
- **Promotional Pricing**: Set promotional pricing campaigns and special offers
- **Price History**: Track pricing changes and customer price history

## 📋 Sales Order Management

### Order Processing Workflow

#### Order Entry
- **Multi-channel Order Entry**: Accept orders through web, email, phone, and EDI
- **Quick Order Entry**: Fast order entry for repeat customers and standard items
- **Order Templates**: Create and use order templates for common orders
- **Copy Previous Orders**: Copy and modify previous orders for efficiency
- **Real-time Validation**: Validate orders against inventory and customer data

#### Order Validation
- **Inventory Availability**: Real-time inventory availability checking
- **Credit Limit Validation**: Automatic credit limit checking during order entry
- **Pricing Validation**: Validate customer pricing and discount eligibility
- **Technical Validation**: Ensure product specifications meet customer requirements
- **Regulatory Validation**: Ensure compliance with regulatory requirements

### Order Management Features

#### Order Tracking
- **Real-time Status**: Track order status throughout fulfillment process
- **Shipment Tracking**: Track shipments and delivery status
- **Exception Management**: Identify and manage order exceptions and delays
- **Customer Notifications**: Automated customer notifications for order status changes
- **Document Management**: Manage all order-related documents and communications

#### Order Modifications
- **Change Management**: Handle customer-requested order changes
- **Cancellation Processing**: Process order cancellations with appropriate approvals
- **Rush Order Handling**: Expedite urgent orders through priority processing
- **Partial Shipments**: Manage partial shipments and backorders
- **Return Processing**: Handle customer returns and exchanges

## 🚚 Delivery Management

### Delivery Planning

#### Route Optimization
- **Route Planning**: Optimize delivery routes for efficiency and cost
- **Load Planning**: Optimize vehicle loading for maximum efficiency
- **Delivery Scheduling**: Schedule deliveries based on customer preferences
- **Driver Assignment**: Assign drivers and vehicles for optimal delivery
- **Real-time Tracking**: Track deliveries in real-time with GPS integration

#### Delivery Execution
- **Proof of Delivery**: Capture electronic proof of delivery with signatures
- **Delivery Confirmation**: Confirm delivery completion with customer acknowledgment
- **Delivery Photos**: Take photos of delivered products for verification
- **Exception Reporting**: Report delivery exceptions and issues
- **Customer Communication**: Communicate delivery status to customers

### Delivery Performance

#### Delivery Metrics
- **On-time Delivery Rate**: Track percentage of deliveries made on time
- **Delivery Accuracy**: Measure accuracy of delivered quantities and products
- **Customer Satisfaction**: Measure customer satisfaction with delivery service
- **Delivery Cost**: Track delivery costs per order and per customer
- **Driver Performance**: Monitor driver performance and efficiency

#### Delivery Optimization
- **Route Efficiency**: Continuously optimize delivery routes for cost reduction
- **Vehicle Utilization**: Maximize vehicle utilization and capacity
- **Delivery Window Optimization**: Optimize delivery windows for customer convenience
- **Fuel Efficiency**: Monitor and improve fuel efficiency in delivery operations
- **Environmental Impact**: Reduce environmental impact of delivery operations
