# Billing & Invoicing Features

## Overview

The Billing & Invoicing system provides comprehensive tools for managing customer billing, payment processing, financial reporting, and revenue recognition. This includes invoice generation, payment tracking, credit management, automated billing workflows, and integration with accounting systems.

## 📋 Invoice Management

### Invoice Generation

#### Automated Invoice Creation
- **Sales Order Integration**: Automatic invoice generation from approved sales orders
- **Contract Billing**: Generate invoices based on contract terms and milestones
- **Recurring Billing**: Set up automated recurring invoices for subscription services
- **Progress Billing**: Generate progress invoices for long-term projects
- **Consolidated Invoicing**: Combine multiple orders into single invoices

#### Invoice Customization
- **Template Management**: Create and manage professional invoice templates
- **Branding**: Customize invoices with company branding and logos
- **Multi-language Support**: Generate invoices in multiple languages
- **Currency Support**: Handle multi-currency invoicing for international customers
- **Tax Configuration**: Configure tax calculations based on customer location

### Invoice Processing Workflow

#### Invoice Validation
- **Data Validation**: Validate all invoice data for accuracy and completeness
- **Pricing Verification**: Verify customer pricing and discount applications
- **Tax Calculation**: Automatically calculate taxes based on customer location
- **Credit Limit Check**: Validate customer credit limits before invoice generation
- **Approval Workflow**: Route invoices through appropriate approval processes

#### Invoice Delivery
- **Email Delivery**: Send invoices directly to customer email addresses
- **Portal Access**: Provide customer portal access to view and download invoices
- **PDF Generation**: Generate professional PDF invoices for customer download
- **Print Services**: Provide printed invoice mailing services
- **Delivery Confirmation**: Track invoice delivery and customer acknowledgment

### Invoice Management Features

#### Invoice Tracking
- **Real-time Status**: Track invoice status throughout the billing cycle
- **Payment Tracking**: Monitor invoice payment status and due dates
- **Aging Analysis**: Analyze invoice aging and overdue amounts
- **Customer Communication**: Track all customer communications related to invoices
- **Document Management**: Manage all invoice-related documents and attachments

#### Invoice Modifications
- **Credit Memos**: Generate credit memos for invoice adjustments
- **Debit Memos**: Generate debit memos for additional charges
- **Invoice Corrections**: Handle invoice corrections and amendments
- **Cancellation Processing**: Process invoice cancellations with appropriate approvals
- **Rebilling**: Handle rebilling scenarios for corrected invoices

#### Payment Gateway Integration

- **Payment Reconciliation**: Automatically reconcile payments with invoices

### Payment Management

- **Partial Payment Handling**: Handle partial payments and payment plans
- **Overpayment Processing**: Process overpayments and customer credits

- **Payment History**: Maintain complete payment history for each customer
